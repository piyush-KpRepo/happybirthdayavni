# The Red Circle Story

Build a complete, polished, mobile-first interactive birthday website for my wife, Avni, from her husband, Piyush.

This is NOT a generic birthday landing page.

The website should feel like a romantic mini-game / interactive love story, where Avni has to solve small clues, enter passcodes, click hidden objects, and complete little challenges to unlock different chapters of our relationship.

The central theme is:

“THE RED CIRCLE OF DESTINY” 🔴

The idea is that our relationship never followed a straight line. We kept moving away from each other and somehow finding our way back.

The story:

We first met in 5th class.

Avni became Piyush's crush.

In 11th class, Piyush confessed his feelings.

Avni blocked him. 😂

Somehow, instead of ending there, Avni eventually became Piyush's best friend.

Avni went to college.

After she got her second job, Piyush confessed again.

This time Avni said YES.

We were together for 2.5 years.

Eventually we got married.

We have now been married for 6 months.

Important memories include watching Avni experience her first snowfall in Austria, our first international flight together, and our wedding.

Avni's personality:

Funny

Crazy

Cute

Makes funny jokes

Loves mimicry

Frequently does exactly what Piyush tells her NOT to do

Makes a diet plan in the morning and orders fast food in the evening

The website should make her laugh first, then become nostalgic, and finally become genuinely emotional.

1. OVERALL DESIGN

Create a premium, cinematic romantic aesthetic.

Color palette:

Deep burgundy

Dark red

Wine red

Blush pink

Warm cream

Subtle gold

Black/dark charcoal for dramatic sections

The main visual motif throughout the entire website is a glowing red circle.

The red circle represents destiny.

Use it subtly:

As a loading animation

Around important dates

As buttons

As chapter transitions

As a puzzle element

Around photographs

As the final animation

Do NOT make the site look childish.

It should feel like:

“romantic movie + mystery game + personal love letter.”

Use elegant serif typography for emotional sections and a playful but tasteful font for funny sections.

Use smooth transitions and subtle animations.

Avoid excessive confetti, hearts, or flashy effects.

Everything must look excellent on a phone because I will send this website to Avni on her phone.

2. IMPORTANT TECHNICAL REQUIREMENT

Make the website fully functional.

Do not create fake buttons that don't work.

Implement actual:

Passcode inputs

Correct/incorrect validation

Locked/unlocked states

Clickable hidden clues

Quiz interactions

Progress tracking

Photo reveal

Smooth transitions

Animations

Restart functionality

Mobile responsive layout

Use local browser state/localStorage so that once Avni unlocks a chapter, it stays unlocked if she accidentally refreshes the page.

Do not require a backend unless absolutely necessary.

Make all personal content easy to change in one configuration/data section.
